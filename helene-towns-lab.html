Notes
  • Coverage per town comes from a tile check run on 1 October 2026 (data/imagery-probe on the imagery-probe branch). Flights covered strips, not whole counties, so a layer can be missing a few streets away.
  • Esri World Imagery today already shows NC's post-Helene aerial photos (Feb 2025) in most of these towns; Marshall and Hot Springs still show 2022.
  • Sentinel-2 in Esri Living Atlas returned no 2024 scenes, so it is not included.
  • A "?" after a date means the layer's outline suggests it covers the town but the tile check did not test it.
  • Mountains are drawn at true height on this page.
