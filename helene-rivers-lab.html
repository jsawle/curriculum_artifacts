About this lab ·

19 rivers from NHDPlus, drawn upstream to downstream. Where a USGS gauge on the same river measured the flow (and the river is a similar size there), the line is cyan and widens with the flow, and the arrows speed up as the river rises above its usual flow.

Where no gauge fits, such as the North Toe through Spruce Pine, the upper Broad River through Chimney Rock (the Rocky Broad), the Cane and the Green, the river is grey and its arrows move at a steady speed: it shows which way the water went, not how much.

Arrow speed is a drawing, not the real speed of the water. The app now uses the same rivers file.
