    Today's line is measured from the elevation data. The earlier stages are reconstructions: the depth of sediment under the floor is taken from USGS drilling (a few hundred metres here, up to about 600 m further east), the Tioga ice limit is marked by moraines that still exist, and the Sherwin ice limit and the shape of the pre-glacial canyon are inferred and less certain. BP means years before present, counted from 1950.
