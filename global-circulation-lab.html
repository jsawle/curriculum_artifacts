    Data sources

    • Wind: Global Average Wind Speeds by Month and Altitude — ERA5 reanalysis, Copernicus Climate Change Service / ECMWF, published by Esri.
    • Average rainfall: Average Global Precipitation — generated using Copernicus Climate Change Service information (ERA5, 1981–2010).
    • Monthly rainfall: NASA GPM IMERG (Huffman et al.), Esri Living Atlas — subscriber content.
    • Land cover: Sentinel-2 10 m Land Use/Land Cover — Esri and Impact Observatory (CC BY 4.0).
    • Cells, surface winds and sunlight: simplified models written for this app (see the versions table), not measured data.
    • Basemap and elevation: Esri.
