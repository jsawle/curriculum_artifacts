    About Earth Structure Lab

    An experiment in using ArcGIS 3D (global scene, see-through ground, underground camera, mesh shapes, slice and dimension analysis) to show the structure of the Earth.

    Data sources

    • Layer depths: Preliminary Reference Earth Model (Dziewonski & Anderson, 1981), rounded. The crust uses a 35 km average (real range about 5–70 km).
    • Temperatures and facts: National Geographic Education ("Core"); Earle, Physical Geology, 9.2 "The Temperature of Earth's Interior".
    • Earthquakes: USGS real-time feed, magnitude 4.5+, past 30 days.
    • Basemap and elevation: Esri.
