About Earth Structure Lab

An experiment in using ArcGIS 3D (global scene, transparent ground, underground navigation, mesh geometry and slice analysis) to teach the structure of the Earth.

Data sources

  • Layer depths: Preliminary Reference Earth Model (Dziewonski & Anderson, 1981), rounded. Crust uses a 35 km average (real range about 5–70 km).
  • Earthquakes: USGS real-time feed, magnitude 4.5+, past 30 days.
  • Basemap and elevation: Esri.
